# Module 2 
## CISSP's 8 Security Domains

### 1. Security Risk and Management: 
    Focuses on security goals, objectives, compliance, risk mitigation, business continuity and the law
    Ex: Updating health policies to match federal requirements 

### 2. Asset Security
    Focuses on securing digital and physical assets. Also related to storage, maintenance, retention and destruction of data
    Ex: Destroying old equipment to ensure data is not breached


### 3. Security Architecture and Engineering
    Optimizing data security by ensuring effective tools, systems and processes are in place.
    Ex: Conifguring a firewall

### 4. Communication and Netowrk security
    Focuses on managing and securing physical netowrks and wireless comuunications.
    Ex: Creating network policies to mitiage data exposure for employees connecting to public hotspot

### 5. Identity and Access Management
    Keeps data secure by ensuring users follow established policies to control and manage physical assets, like office spaces, and logical assets, such as network and applications
    Ex: Setting up employee key card access to buildings

### 6. Security Assessment and testing
    Conducting security control testing, collecting and analyzing data, and conducting security audits to monitor risks, threats, and vulnerabilities
    Ex: Access to payroll info is limited to specific employess

### 7. Security operations
    Focuses on conducting investigations and implementing preventiative measures
    Ex: using policies to stop an unknown device that has connected to your network

### 8. Software Development Security
    USes secure coding practices which are a set of recommended guidelines that are used to create secure applications and services
    Ex: A team makes new app, we are asked to create security policies

# Understanding Attackers

## Threat Actor Types

### 1. Advanced persistent threats
    - Experts in accessing org's network without authorization. 
    - They research their target and remain undetected
    - Intentions can be:
        - Damaging critical infrastructure (Power grid, natural resources)
        - Gaining access to intellectual property (trade secrets, patents)

### 2. Insider Threats
    - Authorized users who abuses their access to obtain data that might harm the org.
    Intentions can be:
        - Sabotage
        - Corruption
        - Espionage
        - Unauthorized data access/leaks

### 3. Hackivists
    - Threat actors driven by political agenda.
    - Abuse digital technology to accoplish goals such as:
        - Demontrations
        - Propaganda
        - Social change campaigns
        - Fame

# Hacker Types
    - Hacker is someone who uses computers to gain accesess to systems, netorks or data. Based on skill they can be beginners or advanced professionals.
    Three Types:
        - Authorized Hackers (Ethical Hackers):
            Follow code of Ethics and adheere to the law to conduct organizational risk evaluations.
            Help with safeguarding people and orgs from malicious threat actors
        - Semi-authorized Hackers (Considered reaserchers):
            Look for vulnerabilities but don't take advantage of them if they find.
        - Unauthorized Hackers(Unethical Hackers):
            Malicious thrat actors who do not follow or respect the law.
            Goal is to sell confidential data for financial gain.
            
 **Note:** There are also hackers who consider themselves vigilantes. Their main goal is to protect the world from unethical hackers.

### 4 Compoenents of frameworks

    1. Identifying and Documenting Goals
    2. Setting guidelines to achieve security goals
    3. Implementing strong security processes
    4. Monitoring and communicating results

### Security Controls
    Safeguards designed to reduce specific security risks 

## CIA Triad
    - Confidentiality:
        Only authorized users can acces specific assets or data
    - Integrity:
        Data is correct, authentic, and reliable
    - Availability:
        Data is accessible to those who are authorized to access it
